Item titles aren't displayed on the page. The title is hidden away in the link tooltip, meaning you have to hover over each item or open it.

This script displays the item's title under the photo, above the rest of the details. It works on your own profile and on other members' profiles, and keeps up with items as they load in when you scroll.

Applies to profile pages on [vinted.co.uk](https://www.vinted.co.uk/).

Untested on other Vinted sites.
